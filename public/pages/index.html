ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Bringing Genetic Data Into Focus

CancerInsight is a web-based cancer mutation prediction system designed to explore the relationship between genetic mutations and different cancer types.

The platform allows users to submit mutation information, explore existing patient mutation profiles and investigate patterns within cancer-related genetic data.

By integrating database management and predictive analytics, the system aims to make complex mutation information easier to explore and understand.
